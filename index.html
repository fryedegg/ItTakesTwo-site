Day-one tests for the cleave plan

Here's what the sources and Marc's experience say so far. Forever hasn't launched, so each one still gets a quick check on the first real pull.

Does Thunder Clap, a stance change or a shout cancel the spin?

No concrete answer found

Nothing found tests these three directly. The Classic-era Warcraft Wiki page says the proc can be countered by cancelling its Bladestorm buff, while a 2006 player write-up says you can't turn it off. Until you've tested it, assume pressing things mid-spin may not be safe.

  • Press Thunder Clap mid-spin, then a shout, then a stance change. Note which ones end the spin.
  • Try right-clicking the Bladestorm buff to cancel it.

Do Windfury extra attacks roll the Ravager proc?

Likely yes

Marc remembers it working, and a Classic player who leveled with the axe says the same. Reports disagree on whether the spin's own hits trigger Windfury. Forever made Windfury a party totem buff, so this could have changed.

  • Stand in Nicky's Windfury Totem and watch for a spin starting right after an extra attack.

Is the spin capped at a target count?

Marc's call: no cap

Marc's experience is that everything in melee range gets hit. The tooltip says all nearby enemies, a 2006 player write-up agrees, and no source gives a limit. Whirlwind and Thunder Clap's limit of 4 doesn't appear to apply to it.

  • On a pull of 5 or more, confirm in the combat log that more than 4 targets take each spin tick.

Thirty-second rule, updated

A dungeon visit earns its slot through quests, a class unlock, Legacy credit, or a named item. Mob XP no longer counts as a reason. Open-world kill sessions earn their slot only with a rested bar and food active.

If the next activity provides two or more of shared quest progress, a class unlock, a major gear target or Legacy credit, it deserves priority. If it provides one minor upgrade plus long travel, leave it optional. Ravager and Nicky's class weapon package get their own deliberate sessions.

Five-minute start

  • Compare levels, quest log and objectives. Pick one goal each; use the same active quest step where possible.
  • Repair; clear bag space; refill drink, buff food and emergency supplies. Check both trainers if an important level was reached.
  • Apply current food and available camp benefits. Use the Sleeping Bag during a real break once obtained.
  • For a dungeon: inspect both logs and prerequisites; confirm the party intends to do the side objectives and escorts.

Five-minute finish

  • Park in an inn or city, or log out at the tent where you quest. Mail excess materials and bank valuable gear.
  • Write the next exact action in the session log. Note missing quests, key holders and any gear targets worth repeating.

Time estimates are budgets, not measured Forever clear times. Queue, travel, launch congestion, skill and group composition can change them. If a group is not forming promptly, keep questing while recruiting.

Before the first shared evening

Your races begin far apart. Recommended: finish racial starter areas through roughly 10 separately, with Nicky completing her Tauren Earth quest before leaving Mulgore. Meet at Crossroads. Moving one character earlier is possible, but the travel and different class quests make it less efficient.

Acquire flight paths in Thunder Bluff, Crossroads, Ratchet and Orgrimmar when your route passes them. Later add Camp Taurajo, Sun Rock Retreat, Splintertree/Zoram'gar, Undercity and Tarren Mill. Set your hearth to the current quest hub and update it when changing regions.

The uncomfortable part first

Forever's beta cut dungeon mob XP by roughly three quarters and only gave back 20% of that. It also trimmed XP from green open-world mobs. Dungeon quests still pay about double their Classic XP. So the guide's "one prepared full-quest run per dungeon" rule is right. But Ravager cleave sessions in old dungeons will be slow XP, and a Protection Warrior using a two-hander switches off most of Forever's new Protection kit. Operation Ravager v2 rebuilds the Ravager plan around that. [2,3,4,9]
